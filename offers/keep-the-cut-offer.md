# Booked Solid — by Studio Bea Sophia
### Campaign hook: "Ditch the 20%"

A recurring-revenue membership that gets local salons/beauty businesses off
commission-heavy booking marketplaces (Fresha et al.) and onto their OWN
front door — a site + booking page that ranks on Google & AI search — then
keeps them ranking with fresh local SEO pages every month.

---

## The validated pain (real, 2025–2026, from Reddit threads)
- Fresha flipped from free → ~£/$220+/mo + a 20% commission on every new
  marketplace client. Owners call it "greedy" and "deceptively expensive."
- UK owners report payouts held for weeks, £140 stuck, no phone number to call.
- The one nobody prices in: your only online presence is a marketplace profile
  that lists your competitors one tap away — and AI search barely knows you exist.
- A two-branch salon in the threads is literally building their own booking to
  escape the fees. Motivated buyers with cash, right now.

## Positioning line
> Keep the calendar if you like it. Own the front door.
> Stop paying 20% to rent your own new customers.

## The automated content engine (what makes the membership low-effort)
1. Scrape pain threads (Reddit/forums/review complaints) for salon+booking gripes
   per niche (lashes, brows, barbers, nails, spa).
2. Auto-draft location+niche SEO pages: "best brows in [town]",
   "Fresha alternative for [town] salons", "commission-free booking [niche]".
3. Each page ends in the member's booking/WhatsApp CTA.
4. Publish monthly per member → compounding Google + AI-search visibility.

## Maths
20 members × £69 avg = £1,380/mo recurring, on top of setup fees.
Content engine cost per member ≈ near-zero once built.
