# Cold Email Drafting Playbook — Studio Bea Sophia

This governs how EVERY outreach email in the CRM is drafted. It is not reference
material — it is the drafting behaviour. Apply it to all `drafted_email_subject`
and `drafted_email_body` fields and all follow-ups.

## Hard rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Length: 50–75 words. Never over 80.** Shorter = higher reply (50–75w ≈ 12%
   reply vs 200w+ ≈ 2%). If a draft exceeds 80 words, cut it.
2. **One CTA only.** A single yes/no or low-friction ask. Never stack asks.
3. **Never open with "I" / self-introduction.** No "I'm Bea and I build…".
   The first sentence is about THEM.
4. **No banned phrases:** "hope this finds you well", "came across your profile",
   "really impressed", generic flattery, "leverage/synergies/best-in-class",
   "just bumping this up", the word "AI", spam words (free, guarantee, urgent,
   act now, limited time). No ALL CAPS, no !!!.
5. **Sign as "Studio Bea Sophia".**
6. **Draft in the local language of the target business** (EN/FR/DE regions).

## Subject line
- 1–4 words, lowercase, mobile-safe (<45 chars).
- Prefer trigger/curiosity/problem-first: `your booking page`, `quick idea for
  [shop]`, `bookings via DM?`. Not clever, not salesy.

## Body structure — OPSA
1. **Observation** — a specific, verifiable thing about their site/business.
   Use "It looks like…" / "It seems like…" (inquisitive, not assumptive).
2. **Problem** — the business implication of that observation (frame via the
   four pillars: Revenue / Credibility / Visibility / Urgency).
3. **Solution** — framed as a result, not a feature; tie to the productized
   build (Seasonal Drop, Job Tracker, Auto-Waitlist, etc.).
4. **Ask** — ONE low-friction, value-first CTA.

## CTA — value-first, not call-first
Prefer offering a tangible free thing over asking for a call:
- "Want me to send a 2-minute mockup of your [seasonal drop / booking] page?"
- "Want me to record a 60-sec walkthrough of the one thing I'd change?"
Only ask for a call as a fallback: "Worth a quick 10-min chat?"

## P.S. line (include one, distinct/human register)
Pick ONE: human line, one-result proof line, safe opt-out line, or a curiosity
line. e.g. "P.S. — if the site's handled internally, no worries, happy to point
you somewhere useful instead."

## Tone for creative/independent owners
Peer-to-peer, not vendor-to-buyer. Direct, short, curious. Acknowledge their
work before diagnosing. Mirror their voice if their site has a distinct tone.

## Follow-up sequence (each email must add NEW value — never "just bumping")
| Step | Day | Purpose | Thread |
|------|-----|---------|--------|
| 1 | 0  | OPSA intro, one ask | new |
| 2 | 3  | value add, no ask | Re: same |
| 3 | 7  | one specific result / proof | Re: same |
| 4 | 10 | same problem, different angle | new subject |
| 5 | 14 | short human check-in | new |
| 6 | 21 | last piece of genuine utility | new |
| 7 | 28 | breakup ("I'll stop reaching out, but if it shifts…") | new |

## Deliverability floor
Bounce <2%, spam <0.3%. Prefer 50 well-researched sends over 500 generic.
Vary send times. Never use spam trigger words. Verify emails before send.

## Video (when available)
Don't lead with it. Earn the reply with text, then send a 60–90s Loom
screen-recording their actual site, naming them, showing one fix.
